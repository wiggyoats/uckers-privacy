Privacy Policy — Uckers

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Uckers ("the app") is a board game. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app.

What we collect

Uckers itself does not require an account and does not collect or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). Player names you enter are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information about your device and your use of the app to show relevant ads. This may include advertising identifiers and approximate location. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can manage advertising personalisation in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads) and reset your advertising identifier at any time.

In-app purchases

The app offers an in-app purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play and are subject to Google's terms. We do not see or store your payment details.

Data retention

The only data stored is on your device (your player names and your ad-removal purchase state).

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact the developer via the app's Google Play listing.
